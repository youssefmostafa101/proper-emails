## Answer the main question first

Write a clear direct answer, then give the context the reader needs.

## Explain the useful distinctions

Use original examples. Label hypothetical examples. Cite verified facts with [1](#source-1).

### Add a subtopic only when it helps

Explain what the reader should check, understand or do.

## Give a practical next step

- One useful check.
- Another check if it adds value.
- A limitation the reader should understand.

## Answer relevant follow-up questions

Answer real related questions without forcing keyword repetition. Do not invent search volume, results or expertise.
