PROPER EMAILS / WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This notice explains how Proper Emails handles information from this website, discovery-call bookings and client intake. Contact support@properemails.com with privacy questions.

Information you choose to provide

When you contact us, book a call or complete onboarding, you may provide your name, work email, business and website details, project goals, current email setup, approval contacts and links to approved brand assets. We use those details to respond, assess a project, arrange access and deliver agreed services. Required intake fields are marked with an asterisk.

Forms and bookings

Client intake is sent through Formspree and delivered to our team. Booking links open Calendly, where its privacy notice applies. If you request the Retention Playbook through our signup form, we collect your first name, email address and consent to send the playbook, retention guidance and marketing emails. EmailOctopus processes the signup and delivers those emails on our behalf. Follow any confirmation request in your inbox. We use EmailOctopus reports of email opens and link clicks to understand engagement and improve future emails. Each marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. After you sign up and confirm your email address, we email the playbook download to you.

Hosting, fonts and browser data

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and form providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs to operate and protect their services. See GitHub’s privacy statement. The homepage and intake load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the browser requests needed to provide them. See Google Fonts privacy information.

We use optional Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits, traffic sources, engagement and link interactions. Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics”; choosing “No thanks” keeps it off. You can change your choice using “Analytics settings” in the footer. We remember this choice on your device for up to 180 days. When allowed, Google Analytics uses cookies and receives technical visit and interaction data. We do not send your form answers, email address or a user ID to Google Analytics, and advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. See Google’s privacy policy. We have not added advertising pixels. The signup form uses Google reCAPTCHA for spam protection; Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply. The playbook popup appears after five seconds on each page load. We do not save popup dismissal or subscription preferences, names or email addresses in browser storage. External websites may use their own cookies after you visit them.

Access, sharing and retention

Project information is handled by the Proper Emails team and service providers needed for communication, hosting, intake and project delivery. Providers may process information in countries outside your own. We do not sell intake information. We keep project records for as long as needed for the engagement, follow-up, legal obligations and legitimate business recordkeeping; provider retention settings may also apply.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete information you have provided, or raise a concern about its use, by emailing support@properemails.com. Requests are considered under the laws that apply to your circumstances and any recordkeeping obligations. Where applicable, you may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Keep access secure

Do not submit passwords, API keys, payment-card details or customer exports. Share only business information and approved assets needed for the project. Platform access is arranged separately using official invitations to yusef@properemails.com after permissions are agreed. This website and intake are publicly reachable; they are not an authenticated client portal.
