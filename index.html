05 / REAL QUESTIONS

Before you
hit send.

Do you only work with large brands?

No. We work with DTC e-commerce brands at different stages, provided the project and budget make sense for both sides.

Can we hire you for just one project?

Yes. You can start with a flow, campaign, or a defined scope. Ongoing monthly management is also available.

Do you work inside Klaviyo?

Yes. Our workflow covers strategy, copy, design, implementation, testing and quality assurance in Klaviyo.

Are the portfolio examples real client projects?

The featured third-party brand examples are independent portfolio concepts, not commissioned client work. ORYN B is an in-house brand project currently in development.

What happens on the discovery call?

We'll learn about your brand, what's working, where retention needs attention and whether a one-time project or ongoing partnership makes sense.
